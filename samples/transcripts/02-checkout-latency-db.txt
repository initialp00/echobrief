Hi, this is Priya with a post incident brief. Around 21:05 UTC checkout requests started timing out. About 40 percent of users could not complete purchases. The payments service was hitting a connection limit on the primary database after an autoscaling misconfiguration pushed too many pods at 20:50. We reduced the max pod count and restarted the payments service at 21:20, and checkout recovered by 21:28. Follow ups: cap the payments pod count, add a database connection saturation alert, and review the autoscaling policy.
